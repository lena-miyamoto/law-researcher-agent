# Contract Analysis Framework

## Clause Categories

| Category | What to Look For | Red-Flag Indicators | Legal Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exclusions (Ausschlüsse) | Risks explicitly not covered; carve-outs from coverage | Broad or vague exclusion language; exclusion of common risks for the insurance type | VersVG §§ 49–68a |
| Waiting Periods (Wartezeiten) | Time before coverage takes effect; per-risk waiting periods | Waiting periods exceeding market norms; reset on policy changes | VersVG §§ 1–15a |
| Coverage Limits (Deckungssummen) | Maximum payout per claim, per year, per category; aggregate limits | Limits below typical loss scenarios; sub-limits that gut primary coverage | VersVG §§ 49–68a |
| Hidden Obligations (Obliegenheiten) | Duties imposed on the policyholder beyond premium payment | Retroactive disclosure duties; unusually short notification deadlines; duty to mitigate without insurer guidance | VersVG §§ 16–22, §§ 49–68a |
| Cancellation Terms (Kündigungsklauseln) | Notice periods; insurer cancellation rights; automatic renewal terms | Asymmetric cancellation rights; short insurer-side notice; auto-renewal with long lock-in | VersVG §§ 74–81 |
| Premium Adjustment (Prämienanpassung) | Index-linking; insurer discretion clauses; adjustment frequency | "Nach Billigkeit" clauses; unilateral adjustment by insurer; no cap on increases | VersVG §§ 69–73 |
| Geographic Scope (Geltungsbereich) | Territorial limits on coverage; cross-border applicability | Worldwide exclusions for travel-relevant insurance; EU-only for internationally active users | VersVG §§ 1–15a |
| Sub-limits (Sublimits) | Caps on sub-categories within overall coverage | Sub-limits that render the headline coverage figure misleading | VersVG §§ 49–68a |
| Indexation Clauses (Indexklauseln) | Automatic sum adjustment; index used; adjustment frequency | Index that does not match the insured risk; no opt-out option | VersVG §§ 69–73 |
| Pre-existing Conditions | Exclusion or limitation for conditions existing before policy start | Retroactive lookback periods; broad "known or should have known" language | VersVG §§ 16–22 |

## Risk Assessment Matrix

| Risk Level | Indicators | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|
| **High** | Gaps in essential coverage; unusually broad exclusions; disproportionate premium vs. coverage; multiple red-flag patterns found; clauses deviating from statutory defaults to user's detriment | Recommend against the contract; highlight specific dealbreakers; prioritize alternative search |
| **Medium** | Some exclusions broader than market norm; elevated but not extreme premium; a few Obliegenheiten that demand vigilance | Flag concerns; recommend negotiation or alternative search; advise on mitigation |
| **Low** | Coverage aligned with market standards; premium competitive; Obliegenheiten reasonable and clearly stated; no deviation from statutory consumer protections | Contract is a viable option; benchmark against market alternatives; note any minor caveats |

## Red-Flag Text Patterns

| Pattern | Significance |
|---|---|
| `ausgeschlossen` | Exclusion clause — verify scope and reasonableness |
| `nicht versichert` | Another exclusion marker |
| `Wartezeit von` | Waiting period — check duration |
| `Deckungssumme begrenzt auf` | Coverage limit — verify adequacy |
| `Anzeigepflicht` | Disclosure duty — check for retroactive scope |
| `Gefahrerhöhung` | Risk increase — may trigger coverage loss |
| `Rücktritt` | Insurer withdrawal right — check triggers |
| `Kündigung` | Cancellation — verify symmetry |
| `Prämienanpassung nach Billigkeit` | Premium adjustment at insurer's discretion — high risk |
| `Leistungsfreiheit` | Insurer released from obligation — critical |
| `Regress` | Recourse/ subrogation — may affect third-party claims |
| `Obliegenheit` | Policyholder duty — check penalty for breach |
| `nachträglich` | Retroactive — check what it modifies |
| `zumutbar` | Reasonableness standard — vague, insurer-favorable interpretation risk |
| `angemessen` | Appropriateness standard — similarly vague |

## Obliegenheiten Checklist

Common contractual duties and their legal framework under VersVG:

| Duty | Typical Deadline | VersVG Reference | Risk if Violated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-contractual disclosure (vorvertragliche Anzeigepflicht) | Before contract conclusion | §§ 16–22 | Insurer may withdraw (Rücktritt) or adjust terms |
| Claims notification (Schadensanzeige) | Usually 3–7 days | § 33 | Delayed or denied claim processing |
| Mitigation duty (Schadensminderungspflicht) | Immediately upon loss | § 62 | Reduced payout |
| Cooperation duty (Auskunftspflicht) | Throughout claims process | § 34 | Claim suspension |
| Risk increase notification (Gefahrerhöhung) | Immediately | §§ 23–29a | Coverage loss or contract termination |
| Premium payment (Prämienzahlung) | Per contract terms | §§ 35–40 | Cover suspension after qualified reminder (qualifizierte Mahnung) |

## Structured Analysis Methodology

1. **Identify insurance type** — determine the VersVG section applicable (life: §§ 149–158; health: §§ 159–178j; liability: §§ 149–158a analog; property: general provisions).
2. **Extract and categorize** each clause using the Clause Categories table above. Tag each clause with its category and the contract section/paragraph reference.
3. **Flag deviations** from VersVG statutory defaults. Any clause that narrows the statutory scope of cover, extends the policyholder's duties, or broadens insurer remedies is a deviation that requires scrutiny.
4. **Cross-reference against KSchG** consumer protection standards (§§ 6, 864a, 879, 6(3)). Clauses in standard-form consumer contracts that are unusual (864a), intransparent (6(3)), or grossly disadvantageous (879(3)) may be void.
5. **Summarize coverage gaps** — what risks is the user exposed to under this contract? Distinguish between gaps that are normal for the insurance type and gaps that are unusual or dangerous.
